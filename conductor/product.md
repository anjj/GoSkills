# Initial Concept

GoSkills is a minimalist Learning Management System (LMS) designed for internal knowledge sharing via video pills.

# Product Definition

## Target Audience
- **Internal Employees**: Company staff across all departments (Tech, Sales, HR, etc.) who want to share and consume knowledge.
- **New Hires**: Onboarding training focus, enabling quick integration and standard knowledge acquisition.

## Content Categories
The platform supports structured courses categorized by departments/internal tool domains, including:
- **Internal Tools**
- **ClickUp**
- **Gitlab**
- **ChatGPT**
- **OpenCode**
- **PowerBI**

## Learner Consumption
- **Video Pills**: Short, snackable video segments (typically under 10 minutes) designed for quick consumption.
- **Real-Time Progress Tracking**: Real-time status updates and persistence of viewing history, ensuring users can resume their learning path seamlessly.

## Content Administration
- **Password-protected Admin Panel**: Content management (uploading videos, creating courses, defining chapters) is restricted to users who can provide the password corresponding to the server's `ADMIN_ACCESS` configuration.
